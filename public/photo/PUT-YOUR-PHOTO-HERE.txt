Save your photo in this folder as:

    me.jpg

That is the only step. The frame on the wall of your house in the game
reads /photo/me.jpg, and until the file exists it shows a short note
telling you this instead of a broken image.

A square-ish image works best — the frame centre-crops to a square, so a
very wide photo will lose its edges. Anything from 400x400 upwards is
plenty; the frame displays at 288px.

If you would rather use a different filename or a .png, change
`photo.src` in src/content/portfolio.ts to match.
